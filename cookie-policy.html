Cookie & Similar Technologies Policy

This page explains the browser storage and third-party content used on the Swimtrain website.

1. Essential preference storage

The website uses local browser storage to remember whether you accepted or rejected optional third-party content. This prevents the privacy banner from appearing on every page load and records your choice on that browser.

2. Optional third-party content

The website can display an iSplash enquiry form and a Google Maps location map. These are provided by third parties. They are not loaded until you choose to allow optional content. If you reject optional content, you can still use direct links to open those services in a separate tab.

3. Google Fonts

The website currently loads the Poppins typeface from Google Fonts. This requires the browser to connect to Google's servers to retrieve font files. Swimtrain does not use that connection for its own advertising or behavioural profiling.

4. Analytics and advertising

This version of the website does not intentionally include Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or other advertising/behavioural tracking code. If analytics or advertising technology is added later, this policy and the consent controls should be reviewed before it goes live.

5. Changing your choice

You can use the “Cookie settings” link in the website footer to reopen the privacy banner. You can also clear site data in your browser to remove the stored preference.

6. External websites

When you follow a link to iSplash, Google Maps, Facebook, WhatsApp or another external website, that service may set its own cookies or similar technologies. Their own policies apply once you leave Swimtrain's website.
